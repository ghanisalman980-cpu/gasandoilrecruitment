What “project director” means on this desk

Oil and gas project director recruitment in Saudi Arabia is a delivery hire: the person who owns a package, an EPC interface or a completions-to-handover window. We also take construction and completion managers on EPC scopes and the interface leads who sit between owner, PMC and contractor. We do not recast every senior title in energy as “executive search”. That work has its own page.

Riyadh programme offices, Eastern Province sites and contractor home-offices all appear in the same brief. The person has usually already delivered a comparable package in the Gulf or a similar owner-contractor system. We will not invent a rank or a salary band to dress the copy.

Permanent-led, with contract only when the package is finite

Default: retain the person

Project directors and EPC leadership in the Kingdom are usually permanent or long secondments that behave like permanent. The fee is a professional search fee, not a race to the bottom.

Contract when the package ends

A defined peak — a hook-up window, a tie-in campaign, a completions-to-start-up overlap — can still be a contract. If the seat is really a wells superintendent, use the drilling superintendent page.

Saudization on delivery titles

Engineering localisation at 30% across 46 professions (in force since 30 June 2026, SCE accreditation, SAR 8,000 floor) applies to designated engineering titles. Separately, project-management professions — including project management director — face 70% Saudization from 14 February 2027 for private establishments with three or more people in those titles. We place Western directors where the capability is scarce and the quota allows. We do not promise a workaround.

EPC mobilisation and when project directors usually brief

Named-package project directors, construction/completion managers and owner–PMC–contractor interface leads are often retained when autumn budgets are still live or when January programmes reopen — the same two GCC waves employers use for senior energy hiring. Defined peaks (hook-up, tie-in, completions-to-start-up) can still be contract; permanent remains the default for delivery leadership that stays. Project-management localisation (70% from 14 February 2027 where the rule applies) is independent of the calendar: we place Western directors where capability is scarce and quota allows.

Hiring-manager questions

Permanent or contract project director?

Default is permanent or a long secondment that behaves like permanent. A defined peak — a hook-up window, a tie-in campaign, a completions-to-start-up overlap — can still be a contract.

Project director or executive?

A project director who owns one package belongs here. Country or functional leadership above the package is executive recruitment. We do not recast every senior title in energy as executive search.

Project director or commissioning manager?

When the package turns over to operations, commissioning manager is the correct brief. This page is the person who owns a package, an EPC interface or a completions-to-handover window.

Should we close a project-director retain before Ramadan?

If the package needs a seated leader before approvals slow, starting the retain in the autumn or early-year wave is usually wiser than waiting until midsummer. We do not guarantee a pre-Ramadan start date.

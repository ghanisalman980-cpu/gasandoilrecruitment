Saudization, stated plainly

A 30% Saudization rate for designated engineering professions has been in force since 30 June 2026. Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation and a SAR 8,000 monthly floor apply to engineers who count toward that quota. We place Western specialists where the capability is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows. We do not sell a bypass.

One-by-one or bulk — still a specialist brief

Some clients need a single wells superintendent who will hold a rig through a campaign. Others need a slate of project and completions leads for a contractor peak. Both are this desk. Contract remains a fit for certain wells and project secondments; permanent is the default for project directors, EPCM leadership and commissioning in the Kingdom. Fees follow the work: retained or exclusive search on senior permanent, and a contract margin on genuine day-rate assignments.

Western headquarters that already use an oil and gas recruitment agency or energy executive search firm in London will find the same British house here, pointed at Saudi demand. The searchable phrase in the Kingdom is international recruitment, not a string of UK city names.

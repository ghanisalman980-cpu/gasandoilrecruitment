Saudization, stated plainly

A 30% Saudization rate for 46 designated engineering professions has been in force since 30 June 2026. Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation and a SAR 8,000 monthly floor apply to engineers who count toward that quota. Project-management professions face a further 70% localisation from 14 February 2027. We place Western specialists where the capability is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows. We do not sell a bypass.

When Western senior seats usually brief into the Kingdom

Gulf programme and headquarters retain work commonly clusters in two windows: September to early December, when decision-makers are back and year-end mobilisation still matters, and January through the start of Ramadan, when new budgets open. Practitioners covering Saudi Arabia often cite September–November and January–March as the strongest approval stretches; July–August is usually quieter for senior loops. We still take scarce seats year-round — including urgent backfills — but we set honest expectations on interview and approval speed outside those waves.

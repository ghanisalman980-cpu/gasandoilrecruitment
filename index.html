Saudization, stated plainly

A 30% Saudization rate for 46 designated engineering professions has been in force since 30 June 2026. Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation and a SAR 8,000 monthly floor apply to engineers who count toward that quota. Project-management professions face a further 70% localisation from 14 February 2027. We place Western specialists where the capability is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows. We do not sell a bypass.

One-by-one or a campaign — still a specialist brief

Some clients need a single wells superintendent who will hold a rig through a campaign. Others need a slate of project and completions leads for a contractor peak. Both are this desk. Contract remains a fit for certain wells and project secondments; permanent is the default for executives, project directors, EPCM leadership and commissioning in the Kingdom.

GETI 2026 (Airswift, 1 September 2026) puts a Middle East reservoir engineer on a permanent package of about $112,247 — not the $122k figure sometimes recycled in older decks — and a Middle East drilling supervisor on $1,098 a day. Those are published market marks, not our fee. They explain why Western headquarters still retain a British partner rather than farming the seat.

Specialist framing · not the generic head term

Senior Western oil and gas specialists for Saudi Arabia — not a volume agency

This is the specialist oil and gas page for the Kingdom: Western seniors into scarce seats. It is not our attempt to rank a generic “oil and gas recruitment agency Saudi Arabia” brochure against firms that already own that SERP.

Discuss a senior seat Wells leadership first

Why this page exists

Hiring managers in Riyadh and Western headquarters still type broad agency phrases. Those phrases are already held by well-resourced panels. Pretending this house will take that head term from them would be dishonest copy. What we will do is name the work: international recruitment of Western senior oil and gas people into Saudi programmes — wells, project delivery, and the scarce technical leads that sit under them.

If you arrived looking for a London oil and gas recruitment agency that can work the Kingdom, that is the trust story. The commercial pages underneath are narrower on purpose.

Where we send people

  • Drilling and wells leadership — the primary lane: superintendent, well-site, completions
  • Project and EPC leadership — permanent-led project directors and delivery leads
  • Permanent commissioning and start-up — secondary, perm only

We talk about the Aramco ecosystem as the system most Western seniors already know — operator, PMC, EPC and specialist contractors — without listing clients we cannot evidence on this page. Mining sits separately on mining engineer and EPCM leadership.

High-ticket specialist fees

A scarce Western superintendent or project director is a fee-bearing hire. Contract assignments are priced on a day-rate margin; permanent search on a professional fee. If the seat is junior, high-volume or fully Saudizable, this is the wrong desk.

Western HQ buyers

Energy executive search and oil and gas recruitment agency are the phrases Western headquarters already use. We keep that language as trust copy for those buyers. We are not building a second site aimed at UK domestic search. For Saudi-facing queries, “international” is the word that matches how the market searches.

Abarrane Limited · Peter Smith

A British international energy desk, not a volume job board

Gas and Oil Recruitment is the public energy-recruitment face of Abarrane Limited. Peter Smith is the public face of the house. We place Western senior oil and gas, mining and EPCM people into Saudi Arabia, and we serve existing GCC markets from this same hub.

What the house is

This is a specialist desk for scarce seats: executive leadership, drilling superintendents, oil and gas project directors, mining engineer and EPCM leadership, and permanent commissioning managers. We work one-by-one or as a campaign, contract or permanent. Fees follow the seniority of the seat. We do not run doorway pages for every Gulf city.

Western headquarters already recognise the language of an oil and gas recruitment agency, a mining recruitment agency, and energy executive search. We keep that language as British trust copy on the home hub. We are not building a second website aimed at UK domestic keywords. For Saudi-facing search, we describe the work as international recruitment.

The company

The company is Abarrane Limited. The published office is in London, United Kingdom. We do not claim a Riyadh registered office on this hub.

What we will not publish as fact

Older marketing has referred to early Middle East work. Until the owner confirms a claim with independent corroboration, we do not present historic operator contracts as established fact on this hub. The commercial story is the desk as it operates now: Western seniors into the Kingdom, honestly scoped against Saudization.

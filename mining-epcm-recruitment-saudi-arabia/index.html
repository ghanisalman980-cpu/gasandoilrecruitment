Senior mining engineers and EPCM leadership into Kingdom minerals programmes. We write to vacancy and specialist intent. We do not pretend the generic “mining recruitment agency Saudi Arabia” SERP is empty.

Honest about the market

Ma'aden-ecosystem and contractor demand for mining engineers and EPCM leadership is real. The broad agency keyword is not an empty results page — established names already sit there. This house will not win that head term by stuffing it into a thin page. We aim at the hire: a mining engineer who has run a comparable pit, plant or study; an EPCM lead who has delivered a minerals package, not an oil and gas title with the word “mining” added.

For Western headquarters, mining recruitment agency is familiar language. In Saudi search, international recruitment of mining engineers and EPCM leaders is the accurate description of this desk.

Seats we will work

Technical and study

  • Mining engineer — mine planning, production, or study lead
  • Process and metallurgical leadership on minerals plants
  • Geotech or mine-infrastructure leads where the scarcity is genuine

EPCM delivery

  • EPCM project director or package manager on a minerals asset
  • Construction and completions leadership on the plant
  • Owner’s team leads sitting over an EPCM contractor

What we will not claim

We do not display client logos we cannot evidence. We do not promise to staff every Nitaqat-sensitive engineering title with a Western hire. The 30% engineering localisation rule (since 30 June 2026, SCE, SAR 8,000) applies here as it does on the oil and gas pages. Western specialists move where the role is scarce and the quota allows.

Oil and gas next door — not cloned

Some EPCM houses run both hydrocarbons and minerals. If the seat is a hydrocarbon project director, use project and EPC leadership. If it is wells, use drilling and wells. The senior oil and gas page is the specialist O&G overview, not a doorway copy of this one.

Send commodity, asset type, EPCM or owner seat, and whether the hire is permanent.

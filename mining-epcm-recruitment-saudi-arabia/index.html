Mining engineer and EPCM recruitment in Saudi Arabia

Honest about the market

Demand for mining engineers and EPCM leadership in Saudi Arabia is real. Semafor reported in January 2026 that Ma'aden has about $110 billion of investment and eight megaprojects on the books. That is Semafor’s interview figure, not a claim copied from maaden.com. Specialist mining recruiters already work the Kingdom — Globe24-7, for one, has published a KSA mining case study — so this house will not pretend the field is vacant.

For Western headquarters, mining recruitment agency is familiar language. In Saudi search, international recruitment of mining engineers and EPCM leaders is the accurate description of this desk.

Live delivery, not a slogan

Ma'aden awarded Bechtel an EPCM contract for Ar Rjum on 22 October 2025: SAR 391.1 million, 39 months (Saudi Exchange announcement). Hatch signed a strategic delivery partnership with Ma'aden on 13 July 2026. Those awards are why Western EPCM houses and owner’s teams still import scarce mining and delivery people. They are not our clients on this page, and we do not display logos we cannot evidence.

What we will not claim

We do not promise to staff every Nitaqat-sensitive engineering title with a Western hire. The 30% engineering localisation rule (since 30 June 2026, 46 professions, SCE, SAR 8,000) applies here as it does on the oil and gas pages. Western specialists move where the role is scarce and the quota allows.

When mining and EPCM leadership seats usually brief

Senior mining engineers and EPCM delivery leads into Kingdom minerals programmes follow the same broad Gulf pattern as other scarce Western seats: stronger briefing activity from September to early December and again January to the start of Ramadan, with quieter senior loops in midsummer. Public project awards (for example Ma'aden’s EPCM activity cited elsewhere on this page) explain why demand exists; they do not set our fee or make those houses our clients. Localisation rules still govern who can be hired.

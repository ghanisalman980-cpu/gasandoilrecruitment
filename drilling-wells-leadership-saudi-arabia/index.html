Drilling superintendent recruitment in Saudi Arabia

We crew drilling superintendents and wells leaders into the Kingdom. This page is for operators and contractors who need leadership on the well — not another junior drilling-engineer list.

Send the mandate Call the desk

The seat we actually work

Drilling superintendent recruitment in Saudi Arabia is a leadership hire: the person who owns the well programme, holds the contractor interface and signs the tour. The phrase “drilling engineer recruitment agency Saudi Arabia” is already a contest between large panels. We do not spend this page trying to out-shout that head term.

Gas and Oil Recruitment is a British international desk. Western-HQ buyers already know us as an oil and gas recruitment agency; inside the Kingdom the useful word is international. We place people who have run comparable wells — onshore, offshore, workover or completions — not CVs padded with a single well title.

Roles this desk will take

Leadership on the well

  • Drilling superintendent / wells superintendent
  • Well-site supervisor and well-site leader
  • Completions superintendent or completions lead
  • Workover and intervention leadership where the well is the product

How the hire usually arrives

  • A single vacant superintendent seat on an operator or contractor programme
  • A short campaign: several well-site and completions leads for a peak
  • Contract secondment where the well time is finite
  • Permanent wells leadership when the operator wants the person to stay

Quota is not a sales objection we talk past

Engineering Saudization at 30% across 46 professions has been in force since 30 June 2026, with SCE accreditation and a SAR 8,000 floor for engineers who count. Wells leadership that is genuinely scarce still moves — on the operator or contractor’s quota, not around it. If the seat is a Saudizable engineer title with a ready national market, we will say so rather than take a fee to fail.

Published market marks — not our fee

GETI 2026, published by Airswift on 1 September 2026, puts a Middle East drilling supervisor at $1,098 a day. That is a published regional mark for a related wells-leadership title. It is not a Saudi commissioning rate, not an Abu Dhabi AED figure, and not a margin we quote. It is why Western headquarters still retain a specialist desk for superintendent seats.

What to send with the mandate

  • Well type and basin or field context
  • Operator or contractor seat
  • Rotation or resident
  • One vacant superintendent or a campaign
  • Start and expected well count

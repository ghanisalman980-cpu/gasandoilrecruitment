Drilling and wells leadership into Saudi Arabia

We crew drilling superintendents, well-site leaders and completions leads into the Kingdom. This page is for operators and contractors who need leadership on the well — not another junior drilling-engineer list.

Send a wells brief Call the desk

The gap we actually work

Live Saudi search for wells leadership still surfaces vacancy and crewing intent more than a specialist British house. The phrase “drilling engineer recruitment agency Saudi Arabia” is already a contest between large panels. We do not spend this page trying to out-shout that head term. We work the roles those pages blur: the superintendent who owns the well programme, the well-site leader who holds the tour, the completions lead who takes the well from TD to production.

Gas and Oil Recruitment is a British international desk. Western-HQ buyers already know us as an oil and gas recruitment agency; inside the Kingdom the useful word is international. We place people who have run comparable wells — onshore, offshore, workover or completions — not CVs padded with a single well title.

Roles this desk will take

Leadership on the well

  • Drilling superintendent / wells superintendent
  • Well-site supervisor and well-site leader
  • Completions superintendent or completions lead
  • Workover and intervention leadership where the well is the product

How the hire usually arrives

  • A single vacant seat on an Aramco-ecosystem or contractor programme
  • A short campaign: several well-site and completions leads for a peak
  • Contract secondment where the well time is finite
  • Permanent wells leadership when the operator wants the person to stay

Quota is not a sales objection we talk past

Engineering Saudization at 30% has been in force since 30 June 2026, with SCE accreditation and a SAR 8,000 floor for engineers who count. Wells leadership that is genuinely scarce still moves — on the operator or contractor’s quota, not around it. If the seat is a Saudizable engineer title with a ready national market, we will say so rather than take a fee to fail.

What a useful brief contains

Well type and basin or field context; contractor or operator seat; rotation or resident; start and expected well count; whether this is one leader or a crew-up; and any Aramco-ecosystem or in-Kingdom entity constraints you already know. We do not need a public job advertisement. We do need to know if you are filling a vacancy or building a campaign.

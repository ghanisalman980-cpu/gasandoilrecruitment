Commissioning management in Saudi Arabia: the seats we recruit

Commissioning management is the leadership of everything between mechanical completion and a plant that runs: systems completion, pre-commissioning, energisation, start-up and handover. On Saudi oil, gas and process assets that leadership usually sits with the owner or the EPC contractor, and the right hire depends on which side the seat is on and how far it reaches.

Systems completion, then handover, then stay

The commissioning manager this desk will search for owns the window from systems completion through start-up and into operations. Construction may still be closing punch items; operations will later refuse a paper handover. The person holds that owner or EPC interface, resident in the Kingdom.

Systems completion

Mechanical-completion certificates, subsystem boundaries, and A- and B-punch discipline so a system can be energised. Completions-to-commissioning leads sit here when the product is a live plant, not a well.

Commissioning and start-up

Pre-commissioning — cleaning, tightness and leak tests, loop checks — then energisation, interlocks, DCS and SIS function tests, and dynamic commissioning. Start-up is first fire and first production or first product, still on the asset.

Handover is ready-for-start-up dossiers, operations acceptance, and punch close-out after first product. The hire who leaves at RFSU is not the hire this page is written for. We place people who remain through first production and stay into operations — that is why the desk is permanent or staff.

Localisation still applies

We do not treat commissioning as a loophole around Saudization. Western specialists are placed where the start-up capability is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows. Three official points matter for a commissioning management hire:

  • Engineering professions, 30%. Since 30 June 2026, establishments with five or more workers in 46 covered engineering professions must meet a 30% Saudization rate. The covered titles follow the Saudi Standard Classification of Occupations, and the list includes Oil and Gas Engineer.
  • Accreditation and wage floor. HRSD’s procedural guide to the decision says a Saudi engineer only counts towards the rate with Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation and a registered monthly wage of at least SAR 8,000.
  • Project management, 70%, from 14 February 2027. Project Management Manager, Project Management Engineer and Project Management Specialist titles move to 70% for establishments with three or more workers in those titles. This matters if a commissioning seat is registered under one of them.

Whether a particular commissioning seat is covered depends on the occupational title it is registered under. We check that against the establishment’s position before any approach.

Timing a permanent commissioning manager retain

Systems-completion and start-up leads who will stay into operations need runway before first production or first product. Many owner and EPC teams open those permanent/staff retains in the autumn wave or early-year budget wave, so the hire is seated before Ramadan slows approvals and before summer leave. Hiring-manager questions

What is commissioning management on a Saudi project?

Commissioning management is leadership of the window between mechanical completion and stable operation: systems completion and punch-list discipline, pre-commissioning, energisation and function testing, start-up, and handover into operations. On a Saudi oil, gas or process asset the commissioning manager usually sits on the owner or EPC side and has to work both interfaces at once.

Commissioning manager or commissioning director?

A commissioning manager owns commissioning for one asset or one major package. A commissioning director usually owns commissioning strategy, standards and people across several assets or a programme. Tell us which seat it is when you brief; if it is a country or functional leadership role rather than a commissioning seat, it belongs with executive recruitment.

Does Saudization apply to a commissioning manager hire?

It can. The 30% engineering Saudization rate, in force since 30 June 2026, covers 46 engineering professions in establishments with five or more workers in those professions, defined by the Saudi Standard Classification of Occupations. Whether a commissioning seat is covered depends on the occupational title it is registered under. We check that against the establishment’s position before any approach.

Permanent or contract commissioning?

Permanent or staff, or a secondment written as staff. We will not lead the site on a contract commissioning model.

Do you publish a commissioning day-rate?

No. We do not publish commissioning day-rates. Abu Dhabi AED figures are not Saudi figures, and we have not verified a SAR day-rate for this market.

Commissioning manager or wells completions lead?

If the person is really a wells completions lead on a drilling campaign, that is the superintendent page. This page is systems completion, start-up and stay into operations on a plant or facility.

Is summer a bad time to retain a commissioning manager?

Loops are often slower in July–August, but an asset approaching first production may still need an urgent staff hire. Tell us the planned first-product window; we will not switch this desk to a contract day-rate model.

Secondary lane · permanent only

Permanent commissioning and start-up specialists for Saudi Arabia

We place commissioning and start-up specialists into the Kingdom on a permanent basis. This is not the lead page of the site. A contract day-rate model is the wrong default for KSA commissioning, and other firms already publish commissioning-agency pages.

Brief a permanent commissioning seat Primary: wells leadership

Why permanent, and why secondary

Commissioning in Saudi Arabia is increasingly a stay-and-own problem: systems completion, punch-list discipline, handover into operations. A fly-in day-rate crew can still appear on a peak, but it is not how this desk prices or leads the work. Permanent (or a secondment that is written as permanent) is the brief we will take.

We also refuse to make commissioning the homepage story. The primary commercial lane is drilling and wells leadership. Project directors sit on project and EPC leadership. This page exists so a hiring manager with a genuine start-up seat is not sent to a wells article.

Seats that belong here

  • Commissioning manager or completions-to-commissioning lead, permanent
  • Start-up superintendent who will remain through first production or first product
  • Systems completion lead on an owner or EPC team, resident in the Kingdom

If the person is really a wells completions lead on a drilling campaign, that is the wells page. If they are an EPCM package manager on a minerals plant, that is mining and EPCM.

Localisation still applies

We do not treat commissioning as a loophole around the 30% engineering Saudization rule in force since 30 June 2026. Western specialists are placed where the start-up capability is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows.

How to brief it

Asset type, owner or contractor seat, planned first production or first product, whether the person stays into operations, and confirmation that you want a permanent hire. Use the contact page or telephone (0044) 203 718 9976.

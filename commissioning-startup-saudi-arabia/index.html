Commissioning manager recruitment in Saudi Arabia

We place commissioning managers and start-up leads into the Kingdom on a permanent or staff basis. We do not publish commissioning day-rates. Abu Dhabi AED figures are not Saudi figures, and we have not verified a SAR day-rate for this market.

Send the mandate Wells superintendent instead

Why permanent, and why this page is narrow

Commissioning manager recruitment in Saudi Arabia is a stay-and-own problem: systems completion, punch-list discipline, handover into operations. This desk takes permanent staff (or a secondment written as staff). We will not lead the site on a contract commissioning model, and we will not invent a Kingdom day-rate to look commercial.

The neighbouring lanes stay distinct. A wells superintendent belongs on drilling superintendent recruitment. A project director belongs on oil and gas project director recruitment. This page exists so a hiring manager with a genuine start-up seat is not sent to a wells article.

Seats that belong here

  • Commissioning manager or completions-to-commissioning lead, permanent
  • Start-up superintendent who will remain through first production or first product
  • Systems completion lead on an owner or EPC team, resident in the Kingdom

If the person is really a wells completions lead on a drilling campaign, that is the superintendent page. If they are an EPCM package manager on a minerals plant, that is mining and EPCM.

Localisation still applies

We do not treat commissioning as a loophole around the 30% engineering Saudization rule in force since 30 June 2026 (46 professions; SCE accreditation; SAR 8,000 floor). Western specialists are placed where the start-up capability is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows.

What to send with the mandate

  • Asset type
  • Owner or contractor seat
  • Planned first production or first product
  • Whether the person stays into operations
  • Confirmation the hire is permanent or staff

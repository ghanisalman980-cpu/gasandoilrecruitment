Privacy policy

Last reviewed 9 September 2026

This notice explains how Abarrane Limited (trading as Gas and Oil Recruitment) handles personal data from people who contact us through this website, by phone or by email. It is a plain-language UK GDPR notice — not a law-firm opinion.

Who is the controller?

Abarrane Limited, trading as Gas and Oil Recruitment. Public face: Peter Smith. Office: Elmtree Court, Elm Tree Road, London NW8 9JT.

Contact for privacy or data questions: info@gasandoilrecruitment.com or 020 3318 2020. We have not appointed a separate Data Protection Officer title on this site.

What data we collect

  • Enquiry form fields on Contact (name, email, phone, organisation, role brief, optional quota notes, and whether you are an employer or a candidate).
  • Information you give us when you call 020 3318 2020 or email info@gasandoilrecruitment.com.
  • Basic technical data the hosting stack may log (IP address, user agent, pages requested) for security and reliability.

We do not ask for payment-card details on this site. We do not run advertising pixels on these pages.

Why we use it

  • To respond to employer mandates and candidate approaches about senior recruitment into Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC.
  • To triage whether a brief fits this desk (employer-first commercial work).
  • Where needed, to keep records of a live engagement discussion.

Legal bases we rely on in ordinary cases: taking steps at your request before a contract (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and/or our legitimate interests in running a specialist recruitment desk and answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)), balanced against your rights.

Retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as reasonably needed to handle the conversation and any follow-up recruitment work that results from it, then delete or archive it under our internal record-keeping. We do not keep open-ended marketing lists built from this form for sale.

Processors and tools

Form and call attribution may use WhatConverts (account identifier 174263 on this site) so we can see which pages led to a call or enquiry. Hosting is on our web host / CDN. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, or objection in the usual way, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). To exercise rights with us, use the email or phone above.

What this page is not

This is not a California CCPA theatre page, not a careers-referral scheme, and not legal advice. Recruitment engagements (fees, exclusivity, candidate terms) are agreed separately in writing — they are not formed by reading this notice.

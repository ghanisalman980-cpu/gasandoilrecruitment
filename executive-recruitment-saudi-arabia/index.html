Executive recruitment in Saudi Arabia

Western country, functional and programme leadership into the Kingdom. This page is executive search — the person who owns the P&L, the function or the in-Kingdom entity — not a restatement of the project-director brief.

Retain the desk Call (0044) 203 718 9976

What “executive” means on this desk

In-Kingdom hiring managers and Western headquarters both use executive recruitment in Saudi Arabia when the seat sits above a single package: a country manager, a vice-president of operations, a functional head of wells or projects, a general manager of an in-Kingdom contractor entity. We search for people who have already held a comparable mandate in the Gulf or a similar owner–contractor system.

Gas and Oil Recruitment is a British international house. Energy executive search is the language Western headquarters already use. Inside the Kingdom the useful word is international. We do not recast every senior engineer as an executive to thicken a brochure.

Seats we will take

Leadership of the entity or function

  • Country manager / general manager of an in-Kingdom vehicle
  • Vice-president or director of operations, wells, projects or HSE
  • Functional heads who report to a Western or Riyadh board

How the search is run

  • Retained or exclusive — not a speculative CV drop
  • Confidential approach where the incumbent must not see the brief
  • Permanent or a long secondment that behaves like permanent

Quota still governs the hire

Engineering localisation of 30% across 46 professions (SCE accreditation; SAR 8,000 floor) has been in force since 30 June 2026. Project-management professions move to 70% Saudization from 14 February 2027. An executive title does not suspend those rules. We place Western leaders where the mandate is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows.

Not the neighbouring lanes

A project director who owns one package belongs on oil and gas project director recruitment. A wells superintendent belongs on drilling superintendent recruitment. Mining and EPCM leadership sits on mining engineer and EPCM recruitment. This page stays on executive and leadership hiring into KSA.

Western headquarters that already buy energy executive search can treat this as the Saudi leadership lane of the same British house. Send the mandate, the reporting line, and whether the person stays in-Kingdom.

What “executive” means on this desk

In-Kingdom hiring managers and Western headquarters both use executive recruitment in Saudi Arabia when the seat sits above a single package: a country manager, a vice-president of operations, a functional head of wells or projects, a general manager of an in-Kingdom contractor entity. We search for people who have already held a comparable mandate in the Gulf or a similar owner–contractor system.

Gas and Oil Recruitment is a British international house. Energy executive search is the language Western headquarters already use. Inside the Kingdom the useful word is international. We do not recast every senior engineer as an executive to thicken a brochure.

Quota still governs the hire

Engineering localisation of 30% across 46 professions (SCE accreditation; SAR 8,000 floor) has been in force since 30 June 2026. Project-management professions move to 70% Saudization from 14 February 2027. An executive title does not suspend those rules. We place Western leaders where the mandate is scarce and the establishment’s quota allows.

Timing a retained executive search into Saudi Arabia

Country, functional and programme leadership briefs often land in the September–early December wave (still-live budgets, desire to seat people before year-end) and again from January to the start of Ramadan (new budgets). Confidential approaches and board calendars stretch; starting a retain in midsummer usually means slower loops, not a closed market. An executive title still does not suspend Saudization — timing only affects how fast a shortlist can move.

How a retained executive search into Saudi Arabia runs on this desk

We do not publish placement counts or client names. What we can show is how the work is done, so a Western headquarters or an in-Kingdom board can judge the method before it retains.

  1. Mandate call. Entity or function, reporting line, in-Kingdom or FIFO, and the quota headroom you already know. If the seat is really a project-director or superintendent brief, we say so and point you to that lane.
  2. Localisation check first. We test the seat against engineering localisation (30% across 46 professions, SCE accreditation, SAR 8,000 floor, in force since 30 June 2026) and the 70% project-management Saudization step from 14 February 2027 before any approach is made.
  3. Confidential market map. Named approaches only to leaders who have held a comparable mandate in the Gulf or a similar owner–contractor system. No CV drop, no advertised shortlist.
  4. Shortlist with written reasoning. Each candidate comes with why they fit the mandate, what they have not done, and what they would need in the Kingdom.
  5. Offer and mobilisation. We stay on the brief through offer, notice and arrival, and tell you early if the timing will slip.
